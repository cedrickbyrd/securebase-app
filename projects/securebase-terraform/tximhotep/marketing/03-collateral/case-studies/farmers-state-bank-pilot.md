# Case Study: Farmers State Bank Compliance Pilot
## Automated FFIEC Posture Management via SecureBase

### Executive Summary
Farmers State Bank (FSB), a regional financial institution, sought to modernize its FFIEC compliance audit process. Facing increasing regulatory scrutiny and the limitation of manual evidence collection, FSB partnered with SecureBase to deploy a continuous, AWS-native compliance landing zone. This pilot demonstrated the ability to replace manual audit preparation with automated, real-time evidence generation.

### The Challenge
* **Manual Friction:** Compliance teams spent excessive hours gathering evidence across fragmented cloud environments to satisfy FFIEC IT Handbook and Cybersecurity Assessment Tool (CAT) requirements.
* **Audit Readiness:** Lack of real-time visibility into infrastructure drift created "audit crunch" cycles, increasing operational risk.
* **Procurement Speed:** Need for a streamlined path to adopt partner-validated security tools within the AWS Marketplace ecosystem.

### The SecureBase Solution
* **Compliance Landing Zones:** Deployed a pre-configured, compliant-by-design AWS architecture, enabling FSB to spin up secure environments that meet stringent banking standards by default.
* **Automated Evidence Collection:** Integrated real-time infrastructure telemetry directly into FFIEC-aligned compliance dashboards, replacing manual spreadsheets with continuous verification snapshots.
* **AWS Partner Integration:** Utilized Private Offers via AWS Marketplace to accelerate procurement and align with FSB’s existing enterprise cloud relationship.

### Pilot Results
* **Audit Transformation:** Transitioned from reactive, cyclical audit preparation to a continuous, proactive posture management model.
* **Operational Efficiency:** Significant reduction in engineering and compliance labor hours dedicated to regulatory reporting.
* **Risk Reporting:** Enhanced board-level reporting transparency through automated, validated security posture data.

### Strategic Impact
The pilot validated that SecureBase's automated compliance jumpstart loop is a viable solution for mid-sized financial institutions looking to satisfy regulatory obligations without the overhead of enterprise-level compliance platforms.
