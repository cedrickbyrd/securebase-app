# LinkedIn CISO Outreach Campaign Sequence

## Campaign Overview
* **Objective:** Book 15-Minute Cloud Compliance Readiness Audits with CISOs/CSOs to identify infrastructure gaps, convert leads into Paid Proof of Concepts (POCs) ($2.5k–$5k), or fulfill via AWS Marketplace Private Offers.
* **Target Audience:** CISOs, CSOs, VPs of Infrastructure, and Heads of Information Security at mid-market fintech, banking, healthtech, and regulated SaaS companies (50–1,000 employees).
* **Core Pitch:** Transition from passive GRC scanning to automated, audit-ready AWS landing zones in days rather than months.

---

## Campaign Execution Matrix

```
[Touch 1: Pattern Interrupt & Audit Offer] ──(3 Business Days)──► [Touch 2: Business & Speed Angle] ──(4 Business Days)──► [Touch 3: Break-Up / Low-Friction Value]
```
